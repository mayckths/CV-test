# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, deployed on GitHub Pages from the `claude/serene-hopper-taakad` branch. Three.js and GSAP load from pinned CDN versions.

## Users

Hiring managers and design leads (heads of design, design directors, VPs) evaluating Mayck Cuellar for a Lead or Staff individual contributor product design role. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or an application, usually between other candidates, and judge craft, product thinking and evidence of impact in a few minutes.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that proves Mayck designs AI and fintech products end to end and prototypes them in production code. Success is a hiring manager messaging Mayck on LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A Lead Product Designer who shipped a live AI assistant (Ask Novo) and builds in the product's own codebases, so the portfolio itself is designed and built in code, with a conversational panel and real 3D work from his own art practice.

## Operating Context

Read on desktop during hiring review and on phones when a link is shared. Visitors compare it against other senior candidates' portfolios. The CV (mayck.design and PDF versions) is the companion document.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: hero with role, impact numbers and certifications; scripted "Ask this portfolio" panel; selected work (Ask Novo, Figma DS Componetizer, Novo first time experience, Logitech G); Google and AccuWeather work at Huge; experience; LinkedIn recommendations; 3D and digital art; contact.
- The chat panel is scripted with hand-written answers and must say so; no AI model or API key runs on the page.
- Contact is LinkedIn only; no email or phone on the public page.
- Light (cream) page with dark panels; no separate dark theme.
- Case studies link out to live sites and videos; there are no in-depth case study pages yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual world, chosen by Mayck in November 2026: a cream page of stacked rounded panels with dark text; a plain blue hero with his name and two particle pyramids standing as an M, which become the glass words "I DESIGN" on scroll (after guillaumezhu.com); content presented one step at a time with stacking cards and spotlit dark panels (after awakeagency.dev); soft particles for the pyramids and transitions (after ricardochance.com); 3D pieces shown as one-ink pixel stamps that resolve into the render (after andreas.vision). Particles never sit behind text.
- Logo: the "Mayck Cuellar" wordmark (assets/logo.svg), used in the header and as the giant name in the hero. The hero object is two particle pyramids that become the words "I DESIGN" in glass; the footer object is the same two pyramids in particles.
- Keep the "Ask this portfolio" panel.
- Keep current copy word for word, including the headline and case study text and numbers.
- The animated M (assets/m.*) is Mayck's mark and may be restyled.
- User preference: no em dashes in copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Impact numbers from the CV: the fintech AI assistant used by 200,000+ businesses (figure supplied by Mayck in November 2026; the project is shown as "AI Assistant" and is not named after Novo); +50% activation in the first month after the Days 0 to 30 redesign; 4+ design systems for Google surfaces; design cycle time from 1 month to 1 week.
- Recommendations from John Chang (Senior Product Designer, Netflix) and Amar Mehta (Program Management and Product Strategy), linked to LinkedIn.
- Project media in assets/ taken from mayck.design (stills and looping videos converted from the original GIFs).
- No real Ask Novo screenshots: the Ask Novo visual is a coded mock-up. Do not fabricate further metrics, clients or quotes.

## Product Principles

1. Prove, don't claim: every claim on the page points at shipped work or a number from the CV.
2. The page is itself a work sample: built in code with the same craft bar as the products it shows.
3. Respect a hiring manager's time: the strongest evidence is findable within seconds.
4. Honesty about AI: anything that looks generated or automated says what it is.
